Privacy policy: rclone-backup

This OAuth client ("rclone-backup") is a private tool used only by its owner, Tomas Sandfors, to upload his own encrypted backups to his own Google Drive using rclone. It requests the drive.file scope, which only gives access to files the tool itself creates.

No data is collected, stored, shared or sold by anyone other than the owner, and the app has no other users.
